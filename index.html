Nursing Leadership Simulation

Welcome to University Hospital

You're stepping into the role of a nurse leader at University Hospital. The team is under real pressure: months of understaffing, heavy workloads, missed breaks, and a recent respiratory virus outbreak have stretched everyone thin. Leadership has just received the first round of burnout and workload survey data — and it's your job to make sense of it, evaluate the technology tools available, and decide what to do next.

This simulation puts you at the intersection of data, technology, and leadership. You'll work with real-looking healthcare dashboards, evaluate specific information and communication technologies, navigate data ethics, and make decisions that connect nurse well-being directly to patient care quality.

You don't need to be a data scientist. You need to think critically, ask good questions about what the technology is actually telling you, and lead with both evidence and judgment.

How This Works

  1. Read each situation carefully. Many turns include a dashboard snapshot with data that may be incomplete, conflicting, or misleading. Engage with it before choosing.
  2. Pick the most thoughtful response. Four options each turn — some are better than others, and the differences are often subtle. You'll get immediate feedback explaining the reasoning.
  3. Watch your Leadership Metrics. Five scores in the sidebar track how your choices reflect key dimensions of technology-informed nursing leadership.
  4. Stop and reflect. Several turns include reflection prompts. Your responses appear in your final summary, which you'll save as a PDF and submit to Canvas.
  5. Replay if you'd like. There are no penalties for starting over. Some of the best learning happens the second time through.
